Definition

Your surrounds that make up the semi-local bio-habitats and ecosystems directly affecting you and your locality. A bio-region defines what plant and animal species are around you, what watershed you are within… we can expand it out to foodshed, materialshed, fibershed, etc.

Basically, where do the looping systems extend too, and how is the weather, the available resources, etc. all accounted for and recirculated in this region?

From Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioregion

A bioregion is a geographical area, on land or at sea, defined not by administrative boundaries, but by distinct characteristics such as plant and animal species, ecological systems, soils and landforms, human settlements, and topographic features such as watersheds. The idea of bioregions were adopted and popularized in the mid-1970s by a school of philosophy called bioregionalism, which includes the concept that human culture, in practice, can influence bioregional definitions due to its affect on non-cultural factors. Bioregions are part of a nested series of ecological scales, generally starting with local watersheds, growing into larger river systems, then Level III or IV ecoregions (or regional ecosystems), bioregions, then biogeographical realm, followed by the continental-scale and ultimately the biosphere.

From Cascadia: https://cascadiabioregion.org/what-is-a-bioregion — They seem to believe that HARD country lines are part of the definition, and that human culture is just as important and plant and animal culture in defining a bioregion...

A bioregion is a self reliant geographic unit defined through watersheds, ecoregions, hard physical boundaries and the cultures that stem within them. Bioregion is short for ‘bio-cultural region’ and are geographically based areas defined by a physical traits; land or soil composition, watershed, climate, flora, and fauna; as well as the cultural traits of the inhabitants that live within them, and act upon them. Ultimately, they are defined by the people living within them.

The Merriam-Webster definition is pretty clear: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/bioregion

a region whose limits are naturally defined by topographic and biological features (such as mountain ranges and ecosystems)

Thoughts

Hmmm. I just listened to a long set of podcast episodes about Madonna — (I really like Yasi Salek's podcast, Bandsplain if you are looking for deep dives into discographies of musicians). The path of an auteur like Madonna is full of new ideas, new sounds, new directions, and new haters... This seems particularly interesting in the immediate present (July 1, 2026), Madonna has a new album, Confessions II, and it's back to her dance-pop and electronic sounds (with Stewart Price again)… Madonna is nearly 70, and society's sort of not kind to aging women in particular, but also aging women whose taste is still young (and look). Is it easier to be say, Brian Eno, who is now 78, and whose interest in sonic aesthetics is more abstract and ambient and experimental, does that more easily turn into someone doing new things as they age? or am I subject to the same bias I am criticizing society for???

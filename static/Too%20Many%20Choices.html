Too Many Choices

14th July 2026 at 10:12pm
Word Count: 128
OnMediumThoughts
Medium
https://bjornmeansbear.medium.com/too-many-choices-3beac892aa28

Given limited time and resources (aka Constraint), how does one decide on what to work? You can’t solve all problems, you can’t do all things. What then gets the time spent on it? Leisure? Reading? Music? Work? — and which of the sub categories and individual items in those taxonomies?

I see things and I say “Hey, I can probably fix that or do that better,” but at a certain point there are too many things, too many interests, too much to learn to solve them all. So, how to narrow? how to focus? how to pick out what is really important and really interesting and really fun. What is the utilitarian decision for answering “what should I work on that really matters?”

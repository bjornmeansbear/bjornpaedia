How might we design a chair that gives back at every stage of its life cycle?

- improve soil health, sequester carbon increase biodiversity (sheep farm is a kind of silvopasture?); bio based, regenerative practices - support farmers, give farmers a premium for the value they provide - grow local circular systems. Build sill and create jobs in sustainable production and re-manufacturing - ends as nutrients for the earth. No harmful chemicals. no plastics. only “nutrients” → this is the cradle to cradle biological nutrient side of things.

compact supply chain, material sheds, bioregion based

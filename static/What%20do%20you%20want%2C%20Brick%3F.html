What do you want, Brick?

27th April 2026 at 11:19pm
Word Count: 95

"What do you want, Brick?" was the question that architect Louis Kahn advised his students to start with whenever they needed guidance.

As Louis Kahn says, “‘we have a lot of material around. we can do it one way. we can do it another way.’ It's not true. You can only do it if you honor the brick and glorify the brick instead of shortchanging it.”*

L. Kahn, Lecture at Pratt Institute(NY, 1973) in R.C. Twombly (ed.) Louis Kahn, Essential Texts, Norton, New York, 2003, p.271
